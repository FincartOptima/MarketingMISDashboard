BD Performance — Calls to Conversion

The BD funnel from calls to conversion. A meeting is a lead logged in the BD tracker, counted in the month it was Date Assigned. Each lead is matched to the Workpoint B2C file by email only; a lead whose email is not found is grouped as Unmatched. In Process / Converted come from B2C. A lead's RM, and so its Team, is its first RM (or its current RM when the first RM is on the BD team). Pick an RM in the RM filter to see that RM's flow; ↓ Excel downloads the RM-wise flow. Calls come from the BD Daily Log, which has no team, RM or platform, so they are shown as "—" while a Team, RM or Platform filter is set. The panel under this heading checks the tracker, the Daily Log and the flow's arithmetic, and says how to fix anything it finds.

Call Flow — Calls to Conversion Download the meeting list

BD Employee-wise Flow

The flow above, one row per BD rep. Meetings Joined = GMeet Joined? is Yes. In Process, Converted, Other States and Unmatched split the joined meetings by where the client stands in Workpoint, so they add up to Meetings Joined. Joined % = Meetings Joined ÷ Meetings Scheduled; In Process % and Converted % are of Meetings Joined. Click any column header to sort.

Team-wise Flow

One flow per team, from meetings scheduled to where the joined clients stand in Workpoint. Bars are scaled to each team's own meetings. Calls are not split by team in the BD Daily Log, so they are not part of this view. A lead's team is the team of its first RM; the exception is a lead first handled by someone on the BD team (Himani, Suhani, Kajal, Kunal, Srishti), which takes the team of its current RM. Leads not found in B2C are Unmatched.

Team Flow — Table

Same flow as above in numbers, one row per team. Not Joined, Pending and Not Logged are the rest of the meetings that did not join. Click any column header to sort.

Google Sheet Clients — Mapped to Workpoint B2C

A client is one distinct email in the BD tracker (the Google Sheet), so a client listed twice counts once here, while the flow counts every entry as a meeting — meetings exceed clients by the repeat entries shown on the first card. Mapped = that email was found in the Workpoint B2C file; Unmapped = it was not — a typo, a blank email, a lead not in the CRM, or one created before the period the uploaded B2C file covers. Unmapped clients have no RM or team to show, so they are grouped as Unmatched everywhere on this tab. The figures follow the filters above; the line under the cards is always the whole sheet.

RM-wise Flow ↓ Download Excel

One flow per RM, from meetings scheduled to where the joined clients stand in Workpoint, busiest RMs first. An RM is the lead's first RM; the exception is a lead first handled by someone on the BD team (Himani, Suhani, Kajal, Kunal, Srishti), which is shown under its current RM. Leads not found in B2C are Unmatched. Use the RM filter above to see just one RM. Calls are not split by RM in the BD Daily Log, so they are not part of this view.
